# Versions and tests

What changed in each version of the kit, what has been tested and what has not. Read it when you want to know how far to trust a part of the kit; the setup does not need it.

## Versions

- **2026-11-21, the download path:** the README and the setup cover unzipping on Windows (Extract All), the macOS prompt for access to Downloads or Documents, and Python on a fresh Mac (Apple's command line developer tools) or on Windows (`py -3`). A person who stops the first sitting early still gets the one offer of the hourly job.
- **2026-11-20, third release:** an easier start. The first sitting asks three questions (name, what the board is for, how to start) and offers one connection, the calendar; the person can stop at any point and the setup goes on with what they gave and the usual choice for the rest. One confirmed summary covers the board folder, the private page and its data; the hourly job keeps its own yes. With the calendar on, the setup writes today's meetings to the page itself, before any hourly job exists. The board is written in the language the person writes in. On the first open the page welcomes the person by name with their own purpose, and a Setup button lists the ten steps that are still open: Tasks, Calendar, Hourly job, Meeting notes, Mail, Chat, People, Work areas, Limits, Preferences. A step is finished by saying its sentence to Claude or by typing an answer on the page, which the hourly job or the next chat applies. The board folder keeps a copy of `setup.md` and of the job template for this. No person has been timed through the setup yet.
- **2026-11-20, second release:** fixes from four persona runs. Safety: the page escapes every link address; before, an address copied from a mail or a chat message could run script on the page. Setup: where to paste the line, and what to do when the kit is not on the computer; a short route of ten questions; rules for a chat source the person cannot name, a tracker without a connector, hand-written meeting notes, a time zone other than the computer's, a declined scheduled task, and a person who wants nothing sent at all (a copy-only board). The import of existing tasks gives waiting items the status Pending others and has rules for weekday dates, other people's items and grouping columns. Job: the morning brief is written by the first run that finds none for the day. Scripts: `check` reports a table line that is not a task row, `stale` and `stamp` refuse a malformed table, `export` deletes only task documents, and a status mistake in the config stops with a message. An earlier board takes these with the last section of `update.md`.
- **2026-11-20:** the setup starts from one pasted line in any folder, copies the kit to a working copy first and opens with a glossary of six words. Six more questions: language, direct reports, where existing tasks live, the calendar, which meetings count and the model of the hourly job, 17 in all. The kit names Gmail, Slack, Google Calendar and Gemini meeting notes where it said mail, chat, calendar and meeting tool. Limits raised for a day with many meetings: 5 points shown per group in a meeting row (was 3, now the `pointsShown` setting of the page), 8 points per list (was 6), 8 cards in Now (was 5), 12 items per sweep of mail and chat (was 6). File names are lower case: `setup.md`, `update.md`, `board/brain-template.md`, `job/job-template.md`, `skill/sync-skill-template.md`. A board with the day view takes the new limits with the last section of `update.md`.
- **2026-11-18:** the day view at the top of the page (Now, Unblock others, The day, Deadlines, Focus) replaces the Top three panel, the per-person blocks and the To confirm list; a Copy button on drafted messages; card fields `slot`, `kind`, `person` and `unblocks`; brief fields `dayDate`, `reminders`, `deadlines` and meeting times; an hourly calendar check in the job. A board set up from the first version is updated with `update.md`.
- **2026-11-17:** first version.

A private test page was then published with the sample: the welcome showed, "Open my board" recorded it as seen, and an answer typed into the Limits step was saved with its time, read back from the page's database. - The hourly-job offer to a person who stopped the first sitting early.
- The start of the corrected hourly job from the scheduler, and the permission questions of a first job run.
- Ask Claude on a newly set-up page.

- `update.md` on a board other than the author's own, where the same changes were made by hand.

## Known limits

- The page's own labels (Now, The day, Deadlines, the status names) are English only. Tasks, cards and the morning brief follow the language chosen in the setup.
- The hourly job's limits are instructions to Claude. What is enforced: the row check of `tasks.md`, the run lock, and the permissions the person gives to tools in the first run. Text from mail, chat and notes is treated as data by rule.
- Two runs of the job at the same time: a run refreshes its lock while it works, and a lock older than 45 minutes is taken over. The `finish` of one run removes whatever lock is there.
- The hourly job runs while the Claude app is open and the computer is awake; after a pause the app starts one catch-up run.

Every person, company, product and figure in `sample/` is invented.
